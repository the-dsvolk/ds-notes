# References

## Datasets

- [Snowflake Cyclic VM Demand Dataset](https://github.com/Snowflake-Labs/shavedice-dataset)  
  Hourly VM demand data from Snowflake deployments (2021-2024). Accompanies the ICPE 2025 paper "Shaved Ice: Optimal Compute Resource Commitments for Dynamic Multi-Cloud Workloads". Useful for analyzing diurnal/weekly workload patterns and optimizing cloud compute spending.

## Papers

- [arXiv:1503.02531](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1503.02531)  
  *(Distilling the Knowledge in a Neural Network)*

- [Scaling Laws of Motion Forecasting and Planning](https://waymo.com/research/scaling-laws-of-motion-forecasting-and-planning/) (Waymo, 2025)  
  Empirical scaling laws for encoder-decoder autoregressive transformers on motion forecasting and planning. Shows model performance improves as power-law of compute budget, and closed-loop metrics scale with model size. Uses 500k hours driving dataset.

## Blog Posts

- [Demonstrably Safe AI For Autonomous Driving](https://waymo.com/blog/2025/12/demonstrably-safe-ai-for-autonomous-driving) (Waymo, Dec 2025)  
  Waymo's holistic AI approach centered on the Waymo Foundation Model. Describes their Driver, Simulator, and Critic ecosystem with Teacher-Student model distillation for safe autonomous driving at scale.
